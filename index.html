BugSocial Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

BugSocial ("the App") is a mobile app that lets users photograph and share insect and arachnid sightings with nearby users. This policy explains what data the App collects, why, and how it's handled.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and display name, used to create and secure your account (via Firebase Authentication).
  • Location data: your precise location (via Core Location), used to tag sightings with a location and to show you sightings near you, at a radius you choose (Town/City, State, or Country) in Settings. Location access can be denied or revoked at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Photos: photos you take in-app of bug sightings. The App only allows live camera capture — it cannot access your existing photo library.
  • User content: sighting notes, species identifications, likes, and reports you submit.
  • Advertising identifiers: if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission, an advertising identifier may be used by our ad partner (Google AdMob) to show relevant ads. Declining this has no effect on app functionality.
  • Usage analytics: general usage data collected via Firebase Analytics to help us understand feature usage and improve the App.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate core features: authentication, posting and viewing sightings, the nearby map and feed, and your profile/collection.
  • Photos you submit are automatically screened using Google Cloud Vision's Safe Search feature before being posted, to detect and block adult, violent, or otherwise inappropriate content.
  • Photos may be sent to the iNaturalist computer vision API (a third-party service) to help identify the species in your photo.
  • To show advertising through Google AdMob, and to measure app usage through Firebase Analytics.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, file storage, and analytics
  • Google AdMob — in-app advertising
  • Google Cloud Vision API — automated content moderation of submitted photos
  • iNaturalist — species identification from photos

Data Sharing

Your display name, sightings, and photos you post are visible to other users of the App as part of its social/community features. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Location: control or revoke access anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy > Location Services.
  • Ad tracking: control anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy > Tracking.
  • Account deletion: you may permanently delete your account and all associated data (sightings, photos, and profile) at any time from Settings > Delete Account within the App.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the information below and we will delete it.

Data Retention

We retain your account data and sightings for as long as your account is active. Deleting your account permanently removes this data from our systems, as described above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
